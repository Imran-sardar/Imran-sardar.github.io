Profile

Assalamualaikum, I am Imran Sardar, a physics teacher and researcher based in Khulna, Bangladesh. I completed my B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Physics at Khulna University, finishing the M.Sc. with a CGPA of 3.92 out of 4.00 and a scholarship for my thesis work.

My research is on rare-earth-doped spinel ferrites. For my M.Sc. thesis I worked on europium-doped Co–Ni–Zn ferrites and studied their magnetic and dielectric properties at the Atomic Energy Centre, Dhaka. That work led to two journal articles.

Presently, I am a Senior Lecturer in the Department of EEE at Northern University of Business and Technology Khulna (NUBTK), where I teach physics to engineering students. I am applying for a funded PhD in magnetic and functional oxide materials, and afterwards I plan to return to Bangladesh to help strengthen physics research here.

Research focus

I study how adding small amounts of a rare-earth element changes the magnetic and dielectric behavior of spinel ferrite ceramics, and which compositions suit high-frequency and memory devices.

System studied

Co0.2Ni0.5Zn0.3EuxFe2−xO4

Europium content x = 0.00 to 0.10

Magnetic properties

How Eu3+ content changes coercivity and initial permeability.

Dielectric response

Dielectric spectroscopy with impedance and modulus analysis to understand losses.

Device relevance

Higher quality factors with low losses for high-frequency and memory devices.

Related interests: condensed matter physics, carbon nanotubes, and data-driven and first-principles modeling of materials.
